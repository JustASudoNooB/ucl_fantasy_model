                                            Man City Vs PSG, Atletico Vs Man Utd: Champions League Matchday 2 Odds

                                            Sergey Demidov Head of News Department
                                            Fact checked by: Sol Fayerman-Hansen
                                            Updated: Oct 30, 2026, 6:02 p.m.Published: Oct 12, 2026, 11:13 a.m.
                                            Editorial StandardsWhy Trust Us?

                                            Champions League Matchday 2 packs 18 games into Tuesday and Wednesday, headlined by Manchester City vs Paris Saint-Germain, Atletico Madrid vs Manchester United and Roma vs Real Madrid. Here is a game-by-game look at the matchday, the best prices across six sportsbooks and six bets that stand out, from Atletico at even money to a Viking handicap against Bayern Munich.

                                            The second round of the Champions League league phase is the first one where the table starts to bite. Sixteen teams are already playing catch-up after losing on Matchday 1, and several of them are favourites this week, which is exactly where the market tends to get lazy. The 18 fixtures are split evenly across Tuesday, October 13 and Wednesday, October 14, with two early kickoffs at 12:45 p.m. ET each day and the other seven at 3 p.m. ET.

                                            The price that defines the week is Manchester City against Paris Saint-Germain, where neither side is shorter than 2.20 and the draw sits around 4.00. The better betting value, though, is on Tuesday: Atletico Madrid at even money against an injury-hit Manchester United, and two home underdogs, Sabah and Lens, facing visitors with poor European away records. All prices below were taken on Monday, October 12 and compared across six books; anyone shopping sports betting odds this week will find the gaps between books wider than usual on the outsiders.

                                            Champions League Matchday 2 Odds: The Six Biggest Games

                                            Each cell shows home win | draw | away win in decimal odds, with the best price for each outcome in bold. Best prices for the other 12 games are quoted in the sections below.

                                            Atletico Madrid Vs Manchester United: Even Money Looks Generous

                                            This is the best bet of the round. Atletico lost 2-1 at Liverpool on Matchday 1 but have since won four in a row, a run that includes a 2-1 win over Real Madrid and a 4-0 against Osasuna, and Diego Simeone has a fully fit squad. Manchester United opened with a 4-0 win over Sabah, yet arrive in Madrid without a win in four matches and with Matthijs de Ligt, Benjamin Sesko, Noussair Mazraoui, Patrick Dorgu and Manuel Ugarte all on the injury list.

                                            Atletico have lost only two of their 19 home matches against English clubs, and United have not beaten them in the last four meetings. The counter-argument is that United have lost just one of their last 11 games against Spanish sides, so this is no walkover. Still, 2.00 at 1xBet for the home win is a price that treats the two teams as closer than their current form suggests. Tonybet, 20Bet and Granawin are at 1.95.

                                            BET ATLETICO VS MAN UTD AT 1XBET

                                            Galatasaray Vs Barcelona: The Handicap Is The Only Way In

                                            Barcelona are unbeaten in La Liga, put five past Feyenoord on Matchday 1 and have won five of their eight Champions League meetings with Galatasaray. Galatasaray lost 3-1 at Sporting CP in September and were beaten 4-0 by Trabzonspor during their recent run. Victor Osimhen is only just back from an adductor injury and came off the bench at the weekend, while Barcelona are expected to be without Raphinha.

                                            The moneyline is not a serious option at 1.30. Barcelona -1.5 at 1.71 with 1xBet is the fair way to back them, but Istanbul is a hard place to win by two and that price is no gift. This is one to watch rather than one to load up on.

                                            Arsenal Vs Lille: Back The Margin

                                            Arsenal won 1-0 at Napoli on Matchday 1 through a Martin Odegaard goal and have won five of their last six in all competitions. Lille are second in Ligue 1 but lost 3-2 at home to Real Betis in their opener and travel without the suspended Ethan Mbappe, the injured Tiago Santos and long-term absentee Hamza Igamane. Their record in England is the key number: eight straight defeats away to English clubs.

                                            Arsenal at 1.29 is too short to bet alone, so the value is in the margin. Arsenal -1.5 is 1.81 at 1xBet, against 1.74 at 22Bet and BetLabel and 1.73 at Tonybet, 20Bet and Granawin. The risk is William Saliba's absence with a back problem and the fact that three of Arsenal's last five wins came by a single goal, including the one in Naples.

                                            Sabah Vs Slavia Prague And Lens Vs Sporting CP: Two Home Underdogs Worth Backing

                                            The early games on Tuesday are where the market looks to be leaning too hard on reputation. Slavia Prague are 2.08 favourites in Baku despite being winless in 16 European games, and they are without the suspended Mikulas Konecny and the injured Igoh Ogbu, with three more players doubtful. Sabah were outclassed 4-0 at Old Trafford, but at home they have won seven straight and took all four of their qualifiers in Baku. Sabah or draw at 1.86 with 1xBet, BetLabel and 22Bet is the play.

                                            Lens against Sporting is priced close to a coin flip, and that undersells the home side. Lens are unbeaten in their last 10 European home games and beat Slavia 3-2 on Matchday 1 with two stoppage-time goals. Sporting are unbeaten in nine this season, but they have won only one of their last eight away games in Europe. Lens are missing Abdallah Sima, which is the main risk. Lens or draw is 1.53 at 1xBet, 22Bet and BetLabel.

                                            BET LENS VS SPORTING AT 22BET

                                            Leipzig Vs PSV, Villarreal Vs Napoli, Inter Vs Brugge And Viking Vs Bayern

                                            RB Leipzig have won all three home games this season by an aggregate of 14-0, yet lost 4-1 at Como on Matchday 1 and have dropped three of their last five. PSV have won six of their last seven and rarely play a quiet game. Over 3.5 goals at 1.89 with 1xBet is preferable to picking a side at 1.81.

                                            Villarreal against Napoli, with the hosts 2.29 at 1xBet, is better left alone. Villarreal are winless in 12 Champions League matches and will be without Tajon Buchanan, who has a hamstring injury. Napoli have gone six away games in the competition without a win and have five players set to miss out. Neither side deserves trust at these prices.

                                            Inter at 1.31 against Club Brugge is fair and nothing more. Brugge have won one of their last nine visits to Italy. Bayern Munich are 1.11 at Viking, a price with no betting interest, but the handicap has some. Viking lead the Norwegian league, have won their last 11 home fixtures and have lost only once in 10 matches, while Bayern are without Jamal Musiala and drew 2-2 with Augsburg last time out in the Bundesliga. Viking +3 at 1.77 with Tonybet, 20Bet and Granawin returns the stake if Bayern win by exactly three. It is one for small stakes: Bayern beat Bodo/Glimt 5-0 on Matchday 1.

                                            Manchester City Vs Paris Saint-Germain: A Toss-Up Best Kept To Small Stakes

                                            The game of the round is priced as a near toss-up with a slight lean to the hosts. City won 2-0 at Porto on Matchday 1; PSG beat Slovan Bratislava 6-1. City are 2.32 at 1xBet and PSG are 3.00 at 20Bet, Granawin and Tonybet. Both teams to score is as short as 1.37, so the books expect goals at both ends.

                                            The value here is with the visitors on the double chance. PSG or draw is 1.68 at 1xBet, 22Bet and BetLabel, and a team that scored six on opening night should not be a 3.00 outsider on reputation alone. That said, it is the least convincing position on this card and stays out of the best bets below. For a single game that will draw this much money, the smart move is a small stake or none.

                                            BET MAN CITY VS PSG AT TONYBET

                                            Roma Vs Real Madrid: A Fair Price, Not A Bargain

                                            Real Madrid beat Inter 2-1 on Matchday 1 and won 1-0 against Villarreal at the weekend, but they have also lost 2-1 to Atletico since. Roma drew 1-1 at Fenerbahce in their opener and have since drawn 2-2 with Inter. Madrid at 1.94 with 1xBet is about right. I would want 2.05 or better before getting involved, so this is a pass.

                                            The Rest Of Wednesday: Liverpool, Stuttgart And Villa Are Short For A Reason

                                            Liverpool (1.29 at LASK), Stuttgart (1.31 at Slovan Bratislava) and Aston Villa (1.74 at home to Fenerbahce) all won on Matchday 1, and their opponents managed one point between them. None of those prices is wrong, and none is worth a single bet. Como, 4-1 winners over Leipzig on Matchday 1, are 1.98 at Feyenoord, who lost 5-1 in Barcelona but sit top of the Eredivisie. That looks too short for a team whose opening win came at home.

                                            Bodo/Glimt against Borussia Dortmund (2.14 for the visitors), Real Betis against Porto and Shakhtar Donetsk against AEK Athens are all priced as open games, and there is no strong reason to disagree with any of them. Betis (2.12 at Tonybet, Granawin and 20Bet) and AEK (2.65 at 20Bet, Tonybet and Granawin) both won their openers and are the sides to lean toward if a bet is needed.

                                            Champions League Winner Odds

                                            Barcelona are the outright favourites at 4.99 with Tonybet, 20Bet and Granawin, ahead of Paris Saint-Germain, Bayern Munich and Arsenal, all 6.49 at the same three books. Nothing on Matchday 2 is likely to move that market much unless City or PSG land a statement win on Wednesday. The full schedule and standings are on UEFA's Champions League site, and the latest sports betting lines for every game are tracked on RG.

                                            Champions League Matchday 2 Best Bets

                                            Atletico at 2.00 is the strongest position of the six. The two double chances are the steadier plays, and the Viking handicap is the speculative one. Odds were correct on Monday, October 12 and will move before kickoff, so check the price before placing any bet.
