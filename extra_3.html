                                            Atletico Vs Man Utd, Man City Vs PSG: Champions League Matchday 2 Bets

                                            Sergey Demidov Head of News Department
                                            Fact checked by: Sol Fayerman-Hansen
                                            Updated: Oct 12, 2026, 11:15 AMPublished: Oct 12, 2026, 11:15 AM
                                            8 min read
                                            Editorial StandardsWhy Trust Us?

                                            Eighteen Champions League games are packed into Tuesday and Wednesday, with Atletico Madrid vs Manchester United and Manchester City vs Paris Saint-Germain at the top of the bill. This preview goes through every Matchday 2 fixture, compares the three-way moneylines at four US sportsbooks and lands on five bets, led by Atletico at close to even money in Madrid.

                                            ##tableColWidth#auto

                                            Matchday 2 of the Champions League league phase runs Tuesday, October 13 and Wednesday, October 14, nine games a day, with a pair of 12:45 p.m. ET starts each afternoon and everything else at 3 p.m. ET. It is the most bettable slate of the fall so far, because the favorites who slipped on Matchday 1 are back at short prices and the market has barely adjusted.

                                            The clearest edge on the board is in Madrid, where Atletico are close to even money against a Manchester United team that has stopped winning. The biggest game, Manchester City against Paris Saint-Germain, is the one to be careful with. Prices were taken Monday, October 12 at four online sportsbooks, and soccer moneylines here are three-way: a draw loses a bet on either team.

                                            Champions League Matchday 2 Odds: Six Headline Games

                                            Each cell shows home win | draw | away win. The best price for each outcome is in bold.

                                            Atletico Madrid Vs Manchester United: The Best Number On The Slate

                                            Atletico lost their opener 2-1 at Liverpool, then won four straight, beating Real Madrid 2-1 along the way. Diego Simeone has no injuries to work around. United are the opposite story: a 4-0 win over Sabah on Matchday 1, followed by four games without a win and an injury list that includes Matthijs de Ligt, Benjamin Sesko, Noussair Mazraoui, Patrick Dorgu and Manuel Ugarte.

                                            The history points the same way. Atletico have lost two of 19 home games against English opposition, and United are winless in their last four against them. The price that stands out is -109 at LetsBetMD, with DraftKings and FanDuel at -110. The risk is United's record against Spanish teams, one loss in 11, which says they rarely get blown away. A draw is the realistic way this bet loses.

                                            BET ATLETICO VS MAN UTD AT LETSBETMD

                                            Manchester City Vs Paris Saint-Germain: Great Game, Thin Edge

                                            City opened with a 2-0 win at Porto and PSG put six past Slovan Bratislava, so both arrive with three points. DraftKings has City at +120, FanDuel has PSG at +200 and the draw is +305 at LetsBetMD. That is a market with no strong opinion, and there is little in the two results to argue with it.

                                            If there is value, it is PSG at +200, a big number for a side that scored six on opening night. It is not a bet to press. This is the game to watch with a small stake or none at all.

                                            BET MAN CITY VS PSG AT FANDUEL

                                            Roma Vs Real Madrid: Pass At The Price

                                            Real Madrid beat Inter 2-1 on Matchday 1 and Villarreal 1-0 on Saturday, with a 2-1 loss to Atletico in between. Roma drew 1-1 at Fenerbahce and have since drawn 2-2 with Inter. Madrid at -111 with LetsBetMD is a fair line for a road favorite in Rome, and fair is not enough. I would need plus money to get involved.

                                            Arsenal Vs Lille And Galatasaray Vs Barcelona: Laying Goals With The Heavyweights

                                            Arsenal are -400 or shorter everywhere, and Barcelona are no better than -357, so neither moneyline is playable. The handicaps are.

                                            Arsenal won 1-0 at Napoli on Matchday 1 and have won five of their last six. Lille lost 3-2 at home to Real Betis, are without the suspended Ethan Mbappe and the injured Tiago Santos, and have lost their last eight away games against English clubs. Arsenal -1.75 at -118 with LetsBetMD wins in full with a three-goal margin and wins half with a two-goal margin. William Saliba's absence is the concern.

                                            Barcelona are unbeaten in La Liga and beat Feyenoord 5-1, while Galatasaray lost 3-1 at Sporting CP and took a 4-0 beating from Trabzonspor in their recent run. Barcelona -1.75 is -130 at LetsBetMD. That looks about right rather than generous, and with Raphinha expected to miss out it stays off the card.

                                            RB Leipzig Vs PSV Eindhoven: Take The Over

                                            Leipzig have scored 14 goals in three home games without conceding, yet lost 4-1 at Como on Matchday 1 and have dropped three of their last five. PSV have won six of seven and drew 1-1 with Shakhtar in their opener. Neither team plays quiet games, and over 3.5 goals at -114 with BetRivers is a better bet than Leipzig at -127.

                                            BET LEIPZIG VS PSV AT BETRIVERS

                                            Sabah Vs Slavia Prague And Lens Vs Sporting CP: The Early Underdogs

                                            The two 12:45 p.m. ET games on Tuesday are where the market looks to be giving too much respect to the visitors. Slavia Prague are favored in Baku at +105 with DraftKings and FanDuel despite going 16 European games without a win. They are missing the suspended Mikulas Konecny and the injured Igoh Ogbu. Sabah lost 4-0 at Old Trafford but have won seven straight at home. Sabah +0.5 at -133 with LetsBetMD covers the win and the draw; the +260 moneyline at BetRivers is the bolder version.

                                            Lens have not lost in their last 10 European home games and beat Slavia 3-2 on Matchday 1. Sporting CP are unbeaten in nine this season but have won one of their last eight European away games. Lens on the draw-no-bet line is -114 at LetsBetMD, and that is the side. The risk is the loss of Abdallah Sima to a thigh injury.

                                            The Rest Of The Slate

                                            Villarreal against Napoli is one to leave alone: Villarreal (+115 at DraftKings, FanDuel and LetsBetMD) are winless in 12 Champions League matches, and Napoli have not won in six away games in the competition. Bayern Munich are -909 at best at Viking, who lead the Norwegian league and have won 11 straight at home; there is nothing to bet at that price. Inter host Club Brugge as heavy favorites, at -370 with BetRivers.

                                            On Wednesday, Liverpool (-385 at DraftKings against LASK), Stuttgart (-333 at BetRivers at Slovan Bratislava) and Aston Villa (-147 at BetRivers against Fenerbahce) all won their openers and are priced accordingly. Como are -109 at LetsBetMD at Feyenoord after beating Leipzig 4-1 at home, which looks short for a road game against the Eredivisie leaders. Dortmund are +105 at DraftKings at Bodo/Glimt, Real Betis are +110 at DraftKings against Porto, and AEK Athens are +160 at DraftKings and LetsBetMD against Shakhtar Donetsk. None of the three is worth more than a lean.

                                            Champions League Futures Check

                                            Barcelona are the outright favorites at +400 with Fanatics, with Paris Saint-Germain, Bayern Munich and Arsenal all at +550. Polymarket's price on Barcelona works out to roughly a 21% chance. Fixtures and standings are on UEFA's Champions League site. Anyone betting the midweek card from a phone can compare the best betting apps first, and current sportsbook promos are listed on RG.

                                            Champions League Matchday 2 Best Bets

                                            Atletico is the strongest of the five, and the Arsenal handicap is the one most exposed to a slow night. Lines were correct on Monday, October 12 and will move before kickoff.
