                                            Real Madrid vs Inter, Napoli vs Arsenal Odds: UCL Matchday 1 Picks

                                            Sergey Demidov Head of News Department
                                            Fact checked by: Sol Fayerman-Hansen
                                            Updated: Oct 30, 2026, 6:02 p.m.Published: Sep 6, 2026, 11:26 a.m.
                                            Editorial StandardsWhy Trust Us?

                                            The Champions League league phase opens across three days from Tuesday, Sept. 8, and it stacks the marquee fixtures early: Mourinho's Real Madrid host Inter and Manchester City visit Porto on Tuesday, then Arsenal go to Naples and Atletico Madrid return to Anfield on Wednesday. For Canadian bettors the question is less who wins than what you are paid for it: Inter at the Bernabeu pays 4.80 at Stake and BetPlays but 5.33 at 1xBet, and Villarreal at Dortmund swings from 4.00 to 4.275. Below are the full Champions League odds from four sportsbooks, with line-shopping gaps and value picks for all 18 matches.

                                            The draw produced a first round heavy on reunions. Jose Mourinho meets the Inter side he took to a treble in 2010 in his first Champions League match back at Real Madrid. Rodri's Barcelona will eventually face Manchester City, Cesc Fabregas leads Como into their first-ever European fixture against Leipzig, and Ferran Torres, sold by Barcelona to Paris 12 days before the draw, could debut for the holders against Slovan Bratislava. At the other end, Sabah, Viking and LASK make their league-phase debuts, with Sabah's opener a trip to Old Trafford for Manchester United's 300th European Cup match.

                                            Four operators are priced below, and their behaviour follows a clear pattern. 1xBet post the longest price on the away side in 17 of 18 fixtures and the longest draw in 15 of them. BetPlays are the shortest on the home favourite almost everywhere, sometimes by a wide margin. Tonybet and Stake sit in between and frequently agree with each other to within a few basis points. On short favourites the four are near-identical; on underdogs and draws they diverge by enough to change the return on every slip. Compare the sports betting lines before committing, because on this card the operator is often worth more than the selection.

                                            All odds below are Home / Draw / Away in decimal format, current as of Sunday, Sept. 6. Odds are subject to change; verify before betting.

                                            Where the Four Books Disagree Most

                                            Three gaps stand out. Inter to win at Real Madrid pays 5.33 at 1xBet against 4.80 at Stake and BetPlays, an 11% difference in return on the same result. Sabah to win at Old Trafford runs from 16.00 at Tonybet to 26.00 at 1xBet, a 63% spread that says more about how little the books know about the Azerbaijani debutants than anything else. And Shakhtar at PSV range from 6.00 at Tonybet to 7.14 at 1xBet, 19% on an away side that has lost only two of its last eight group-stage matches.

                                            The draws tell the same story. Lille vs Real Betis is 3.40 at Stake and BetPlays but 3.64 at 1xBet; Liverpool vs Atletico is 4.00 at three books and 4.275 at the fourth; Fenerbahce vs Roma runs from 3.40 to 3.60. On the favourites, the notable outlier is BetPlays, who are 1.82 on Sporting against 1.90 to 1.91 elsewhere and 1.60 on Real Madrid against 1.64 at the other three.                                             Tuesday: Mourinho vs Inter, City at the Dragao and Villa in Bruges

                                            Club Brugge vs Aston Villa

                                            Club Brugge vs Aston Villa (2.43-2.535 on Villa): The early window's most open line. Villa have one point from three Premier League matches, lost 4-0 to Brighton, and are without Amadou Onana long-term, Brian Madjo and Johan Manzambi. Their European record under Unai Emery is another matter entirely: 13 wins from 15 in last season's Europa League title run, three defeats in their last 21 group-stage or league-phase games, and nine clean sheets in their last 16 UEFA matches. Brugge are second in Belgium with four wins from five, have lost only three of their last 22 UEFA home games, and scored three or more in six of their last seven of them; they also beat Villa 1-0 here in the 2024/25 league phase before losing both legs of the round of 16. Joel Ordonez is out. On paper this is the most draw-shaped fixture of the early window. Villa at 2.535 on 1xBet is 10 basis points clear of Stake; Tonybet have set the total at 3.0 with Over at 2.15 and Under at 1.74, while 1xBet's Over 2.5 is 1.724. Prediction: Club Brugge 1-1 Aston Villa.

                                            AEK Athens vs LASK

                                            AEK Athens vs LASK (1.75-1.789): AEK are back in the competition proper for the first time since 2018/19 and have won nine of their last 11 UEFA home matches. LASK are debutants who won the Austrian double last season, their first major honours since 1965, and beat Celtic 5-1 in the play-off second leg to end an 11-match winless European run. Only one of AEK's last 22 group-stage matches was goalless. AEK at 1.789 on 1xBet is the top of the market; LASK at 4.52 there is 32 basis points clear of BetPlays. Tonybet have the total at 3.0 with Over 2.04. Prediction: AEK Athens 2-1 LASK.

                                            Lille vs Real Betis

                                            Lille vs Real Betis (2.20-2.252): Lille have lost only one of nine home matches against Spanish clubs, including a 1-0 win over Real Madrid two seasons ago, and none of their last 34 European matches has ended 0-0. Betis are in the European Cup for only the second time and have lost just one of their last ten group-stage or league-phase games. The draw at 3.64 on 1xBet is 24 basis points clear of Stake and BetPlays and is the widest draw gap of the round. Tonybet's total is 2.5 with Over at 1.82. Prediction: Lille 2-1 Real Betis.

                                            Real Madrid vs Inter

                                            Real Madrid vs Inter (1.60-1.64): Mourinho against the club he won the 2010 treble with. Madrid have won six of the last seven meetings, 19 of their last 21 against Italian clubs, and their Matchday 1 fixture in 17 of the last 19 campaigns. Inter are winless in 13 away matches in Spain since 2004, but none of their last 15 league-phase games has been drawn and they have failed to score in just two of their last 19 in the competition. Kylian Mbappe is one goal behind Raul on the all-time list, and Thibaut Courtois makes his 100th Champions League appearance if he plays. Madrid are 1.64 at three books; Inter at 5.33 on 1xBet is the round's headline underdog gap. Tonybet have the total at 3.0 with Over 1.82, while 1xBet's Over 3.5 is 2.357 and both teams to score is 1.55. Prediction: Real Madrid 3-1 Inter.

                                            Borussia Dortmund vs Villarreal

                                            Borussia Dortmund vs Villarreal (1.76-1.811): Dortmund won the only previous meeting 4-0 last season and scored four in each of their first three league-phase games in 2025/26. They have lost two of their last 15 home matches against Spanish sides and won the last three. Villarreal have won two of their last ten against German clubs, and none of their last 31 Champions League proper matches has finished 0-0. Dortmund at 1.811 on 1xBet is five basis points clear of BetPlays; Villarreal at 4.275 there is 27 basis points better than BetPlays' 4.00. 1xBet's Over 3.5 at 2.357 is the totals angle. Prediction: Borussia Dortmund 3-1 Villarreal.

                                            Porto vs Manchester City

                                            Porto vs Manchester City (1.71-1.772 on City): City's 150th European Cup match comes at a ground where Porto have lost one of their last 14 UEFA home games. City have lost six of their last nine Champions League away matches, most recently 4-1 at Sporting on their last trip to Portugal. Porto, under former Ajax coach Francesco Farioli, have won two of their last 16 against English clubs. City at 1.772 on 1xBet is six basis points clear of BetPlays; Porto at 4.63 there is 23 clear of Stake and BetPlays. The draw is 4.00 to 4.075 and is a live outcome. Tonybet's total is 3.0 with Under at 1.77. Prediction: Porto 1-1 Manchester City.

Viking, Norway's second straight Champions League debutant, are winless in six away matches in Germany. Stuttgart at 1.258 on 1xBet is four basis points clear of the field; the total is 3.5 at Tonybet with Over at 1.74 and 1xBet's Over 3.5 at 1.755. Prediction: Stuttgart 3-0 Viking.

                                            Barcelona vs Feyenoord

                                            Barcelona vs Feyenoord (1.10-1.128): Barcelona have scored 50 goals in two league phases, eight more than anyone else, and have not kept a clean sheet in their last 15 Champions League matches. Lamine Yamal needs two goals to equal Mbappe's record for most Champions League goals aged 20 or under. Feyenoord are winless in 11 European away games and have lost eight of their last 11 against Spanish sides. The moneyline is dead; the total is where the market lives, with Tonybet at 4.0 (Over 1.86) and 1xBet's Over 4.5 at 2.286. Feyenoord at 19.5 on 1xBet is the longest away price. Prediction: Barcelona 4-1 Feyenoord.

                                            PSG vs Slovan Bratislava

                                            PSG vs Slovan Bratislava (1.02-1.05): The holders have won on Matchday 1 in each of the last four seasons, without conceding in the last three, and have played one 0-0 in their last 118 European matches. Slovan lost all eight league-phase games two seasons ago but arrive unbeaten in seven UEFA fixtures. Nothing on the 1X2; Tonybet's total is 4.0 with Over at 2.01, and 1xBet's Over 3.5 is 1.663. Prediction: PSG 4-0 Slovan Bratislava.

                                            Napoli vs Arsenal

                                            Napoli vs Arsenal (1.80-1.872 on Arsenal): The match of the round. Arsenal conceded seven goals in 15 games last season with nine clean sheets, were unbeaten in regulation time, and are unbeaten on Matchday 1 in their last nine European campaigns. They have won seven of their last ten against Italian clubs and three of four against Napoli. Napoli have won two of their last ten in the Champions League proper and lost their last three against English opposition. Arsenal at 1.872 on 1xBet is seven basis points clear of BetPlays. 1xBet have both teams to score at 1.81, Over 2.5 at 1.992 and Under 2.5 at 1.91; Tonybet's total is 2.5 with Under at 1.88. Prediction: Napoli 0-1 Arsenal.

                                            Liverpool vs Atletico Madrid

                                            Liverpool vs Atletico Madrid (1.67-1.73): A rematch of last season's Matchday 1, which Liverpool won 3-2 at Anfield. Liverpool have finished in the top three of both league phases and have won their last six against Spanish clubs in the format, with four clean sheets in the last five. Atletico have never won a Champions League group or league-phase match against an English side (D4 L6), though they have failed to score in only two of their last 24 in the format, and Koke reaches 150 UEFA club appearances if selected. Liverpool at 1.73 on Tonybet is the top price on a favourite that has yet to win in the Premier League; Atletico at 4.825 on 1xBet is 32 basis points clear of BetPlays. 1xBet have Over 2.5 at 1.648. Prediction: Liverpool 2-1 Atletico Madrid.

                                            Sporting CP vs Galatasaray

                                            Sporting CP vs Galatasaray (1.82-1.91): The first-ever meeting between clubs with more than 700 UEFA fixtures between them. Sporting reached the quarter-finals last season and have won four of their last five against Turkish sides. Galatasaray reached the round of 16 but are winless in five against Portuguese clubs and have won their opener in one of their last ten Champions League campaigns. Sporting at 1.91 on Tonybet is nine basis points clear of BetPlays, the widest favourite gap on a sub-2.00 price this round. Galatasaray at 4.125 on 1xBet is the long end. Tonybet's total is 3.0 with Over 2.10. Prediction: Sporting CP 2-1 Galatasaray.

PSV at 1.46 on Stake is the top of the market; Shakhtar at 7.14 on 1xBet is 19% better than Tonybet. Tonybet's total is 3.0 with Under 2.01; 1xBet's Over 3.5 is 2.316. United at 1.165 on 1xBet is three basis points clear of the field; Sabah at 26.00 there is a 63% premium over Tonybet's 16.00. Tonybet's total is 4.0 with Over 2.04; 1xBet's Over 3.5 is 1.724.                                             Slavia Prague vs Lens
