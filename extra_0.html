                                            Champions League Matchday 1 Odds, Predictions and Best Bets

                                            The RG Media Desk Editorial Desk
                                            Fact checked by: Sergey Demidov
                                            Updated: Sep 6, 2026, 11:23 AMPublished: Sep 6, 2026, 11:23 AM
                                            16 min read
                                            Editorial StandardsWhy Trust Us?

                                            The 2026/27 Champions League league phase opens Tuesday with Real Madrid vs Inter and Porto vs Manchester City, continues Wednesday with Napoli vs Arsenal and Liverpool vs Atletico Madrid, and closes Thursday with Bayern's attempt to extend a 22-campaign run of Matchday 1 wins. Below are the latest Champions League odds from three US sportsbooks, betting analysis, best bets and score predictions for all 18 fixtures from Sept. 8 to Sept. 10.

                                            The draw handed Matchday 1 more subplots than any opening round in recent memory. Jose Mourinho starts his second spell at Real Madrid by hosting the Inter side he took to a treble in 2010. Arsenal, last season's runners-up with the fewest goals conceded in the competition, walk into Naples. Paris Saint-Germain begin a bid to become only the second club of the Champions League era to win three in a row. And at the other end of the food chain, Sabah, Viking, LASK and Como all make their debuts in the competition proper, with Como's Cesc Fabregas managing his first European game after 104 Champions League appearances as a player.

                                            That mixture of heavyweights and newcomers is why the pricing is so uneven. The books are in near-total agreement on the seven or eight favorites priced at -500 or shorter, but on the coin-flip fixtures and the draws they diverge by 10 to 20 cents, which is exactly where the value sits on a three-day slate.

                                            Odds below are courtesy of FanDuel Sportsbook, BetRivers and LetsBetMD, so you can shop every three-way line before committing.

                                            Champions League Matchday 1 Odds: Where the Three Books Split

                                            All odds below are Home / Draw / Away in American format, current as of Sunday, Sept. 6. Odds are subject to change; verify before betting.

                                            The spreads to know before anything else: Stuttgart are -550 at FanDuel but -385 at BetRivers, the widest favorite gap on the board. Manchester United range from -900 to -670. Bayern from -900 to -714. On the underdog side, Bodo/Glimt pay +1400 at FanDuel and +1600 at BetRivers, Inter swing from +390 to +440, and Lens at Slavia run from +135 to +150. Liverpool at home to Atletico are -165 at one book and -133 at another, a difference that changes the math on every parlay that includes them. With this many mismatches in one round, keeping two or three of the top sports betting sites open is not optional.

                                            Ready to build a Matchday 1 card? Open the full Champions League market at FanDuel and compare its three-way lines with the numbers below before Tuesday's early kickoffs.

                                            Tuesday: Mourinho's Return, City in Porto and Villa in Belgium

                                            Club Brugge vs Aston Villa

                                            Club Brugge vs Aston Villa (+140 to +147 on Villa): The most interesting line of the early window. Villa have one point from three Premier League matches, including a 4-0 loss to Brighton and defeats to Arsenal and Hull City, and they are missing Amadou Onana long-term with Brian Madjo and Johan Manzambi also unavailable. Yet the Europe of Unai Emery is a different animal: 13 wins in 15 matches during last season's Europa League title run, only three defeats in their last 21 group-stage or league-phase games, and nine clean sheets in their last 16 European outings. Brugge are second in the Belgian league with four wins from five, have lost just three of their last 22 UEFA home matches, and beat Villa 1-0 here in the 2024/25 league phase before losing 3-1 and 3-0 in the round of 16. Joel Ordonez is out for the hosts. A draw is the most likely outcome on paper, and with Brugge scoring three or more in six of their last seven UEFA home games against a Villa defense that has conceded five in three league matches, BetRivers' Over 2.5 at -148 is well priced. LetsBetMD's +147 is the top of the market on Villa. Prediction: Club Brugge 1-1 Aston Villa.

                                            AEK Athens vs LASK

                                            AEK Athens vs LASK (-132 to -150): AEK are back in the competition proper for the first time since 2018/19 and have won nine of their last 11 UEFA home matches. LASK are debutants who ended an 11-match winless European run by beating Celtic 5-1 in the play-off second leg. Only one of AEK's last 22 group-stage matches has been goalless. BetRivers' -132 is 18 cents better than FanDuel on the home side and is the only number worth taking. Prediction: AEK Athens 2-1 LASK.

                                            Lille vs Real Betis

                                            Lille vs Real Betis (+120 to +127 on Lille): Lille have lost just one of nine home matches against Spanish clubs, and that record includes a 1-0 win over Real Madrid two seasons ago. None of their last 34 European matches has finished 0-0, and they have scored in each of their last nine Champions League proper games. Betis are in the European Cup for only the second time and have lost just one of their last ten group-stage or league-phase matches. This is the most balanced three-way line of the round, and the draw at +260 on LetsBetMD is a real consideration. Lille at +127 on LetsBetMD is the best home price. Prediction: Lille 2-1 Real Betis.

                                            Real Madrid vs Inter

                                            Real Madrid vs Inter (-161 to -175): Mourinho against the club that made him immortal. Madrid have won six of the last seven meetings, 19 of their last 21 against Italian opposition, and their Matchday 1 fixture in 17 of the last 19 campaigns. Inter are winless in 13 away matches in Spain since 2004, but none of their last 15 league-phase games has ended level and they have failed to score in only two of their last 19 Champions League matches. Kylian Mbappe sits on 70 Champions League goals, one behind Raul, and Thibaut Courtois makes his 100th appearance in the competition if he plays. The draw is the market anomaly: none of Inter's recent league-phase games has been drawn, and Madrid's ceiling is high, so BetRivers' Over 3.5 at +140 is the angle rather than the favorite at -175. LetsBetMD's -161 is the cheapest way onto Madrid. Prediction: Real Madrid 3-1 Inter.

                                            Borussia Dortmund vs Villarreal

                                            Borussia Dortmund vs Villarreal (-130 to -145): The only previous meeting was Dortmund 4-0 on Matchday 5 last season, and BVB scored four in each of their first three league-phase games in 2025/26. They have lost just two of their last 15 home matches against Spanish sides and won the last three. Villarreal are making back-to-back Champions League appearances for the first time, have won only two of their last ten against German clubs, and none of their last 31 matches in the competition proper has been goalless. LetsBetMD's -130 is 15 cents better than FanDuel on the home side. The Over 3.5 at +140 on BetRivers is playable given both defensive records. Prediction: Borussia Dortmund 3-1 Villarreal.

                                            Porto vs Manchester City

                                            Porto vs Manchester City (-133 to -150 on City): City's 150th European Cup match comes at a ground where Porto have lost only one of their last 14 UEFA home games. City have lost six of their last nine Champions League away matches, and their most recent trip to Portugal ended 4-1 at Sporting. Porto, under Francesco Farioli, have won just two of their last 16 against English clubs. The market has City at -133 to -150, which is a short price for a side with that road record. Porto at +360 on BetRivers is long, and the draw at +300 is sharper than usual. Prediction: Porto 1-1 Manchester City.

                                            Wednesday: Arsenal in Naples, Atleti at Anfield, the Holders Begin

                                            Stuttgart vs Viking

                                            Stuttgart vs Viking (-385 to -550): Stuttgart have never won a Matchday 1 fixture in the Champions League, drawing one and losing three, but they won all four home matches in last season's Europa League league phase. Viking are Norway's second consecutive Champions League debutant after Bodo/Glimt and are winless in six away games against German opposition. The 165-cent gap between FanDuel and BetRivers on the favorite is the widest on the card, and BetRivers' Over 3.5 at -124 is worth more than any moneyline number here. Prediction: Stuttgart 3-0 Viking.

                                            Barcelona vs Feyenoord

                                            Barcelona vs Feyenoord (-833 to -1000): Barcelona have scored 50 goals across two league phases, eight more than any other club, and have not kept a clean sheet in their last 15 Champions League matches. Lamine Yamal needs two goals to tie Mbappe's record for most Champions League goals aged 20 or under. Feyenoord are winless in 11 European away games and have lost eight of their last 11 against Spanish sides, but they have failed to score in only two of their last 15 group-stage matches. That combination is why the total is set at 4.5, with BetRivers' Over at +123. Prediction: Barcelona 4-1 Feyenoord.

                                            PSG vs Slovan Bratislava

                                            PSG vs Slovan Bratislava (-1667 to -2000): The holders have won on Matchday 1 in each of the last four seasons, with clean sheets in the three most recent, and have played just one 0-0 draw in their last 118 European matches. Slovan lost all eight league-phase games in 2024/25 but arrive unbeaten in seven UEFA matches. Ferran Torres, signed from Barcelona 12 days before the draw, could feature against a side he has never faced. Nothing to be had on the moneyline; the Over 3.5 at -148 on BetRivers is the market. Prediction: PSG 4-0 Slovan Bratislava.

                                            Napoli vs Arsenal

                                            Napoli vs Arsenal (-118 to -130 on Arsenal): The match of the round. Arsenal conceded seven goals in 15 matches last season, kept nine clean sheets, went unbeaten in regulation time, and are unbeaten on Matchday 1 in their last nine European campaigns. They have won seven of their last ten against Italian clubs, including both legs of a 2018/19 Europa League quarter-final against Napoli. Napoli have won two of their last ten in the Champions League proper and lost their last three against English sides, though they did beat Arsenal 2-0 here in 2013/14, a match Mikel Arteta was sent off in as a player. Arsenal at -118 on LetsBetMD is 12 cents better than FanDuel. BetRivers have the Under 2.5 at -113 and that reflects Arsenal's defensive profile. Prediction: Napoli 0-1 Arsenal.

                                            Liverpool vs Atletico Madrid

                                            Liverpool vs Atletico Madrid (-133 to -165): A rematch of last season's Matchday 1, which Liverpool won 3-2 at Anfield. Liverpool have finished in the top three of both league phases and have won their last six against Spanish clubs in the format, with four clean sheets in the last five. Atletico have never won a Champions League group or league-phase match against an English side (D4 L6), but they have failed to score in only two of their last 24 in the format. Koke reaches 150 UEFA club appearances if selected. Liverpool are winless in the Premier League after two draws, and the market has moved: LetsBetMD's -133 is 32 cents better than FanDuel on the home favorite, a gap that decides where this leg belongs. Atletico at +380 on FanDuel is the outlier on the other side. Prediction: Liverpool 2-1 Atletico Madrid.

                                            Sporting CP vs Galatasaray

                                            Sporting CP vs Galatasaray (-111 to -120): The first-ever meeting between clubs with more than 700 UEFA fixtures between them. Sporting reached the quarter-finals last season, losing 1-0 on aggregate to Arsenal, and have won four of their last five against Turkish sides. Galatasaray reached the round of 16 but are winless in five against Portuguese opposition and have won their opening match in just one of their last ten Champions League campaigns. Sporting at -111 on LetsBetMD is close to a pick'em price for a side with that head-to-head profile. Prediction: Sporting CP 2-1 Galatasaray.

                                            Want the widest range of totals and alternate lines? Compare Wednesday's Champions League markets at BetRivers and take the best number on each leg rather than building the whole slip at one book.

                                            Thursday: Bayern's Streak, United's 300th and Como's Debut

                                            PSV vs Shakhtar Donetsk

                                            PSV vs Shakhtar Donetsk (-250 to -270): PSV won the only previous meeting 3-2 at home two seasons ago and have won their last three Champions League matches against Ukrainian clubs. The catch: they have lost their Matchday 1 fixture in each of their last five campaigns, with their last opening win coming against Manchester United in 2015. Shakhtar have lost only two of their last eight group-stage games. LetsBetMD's -250 is the best home price, but Shakhtar at +650 on BetRivers is the more interesting number given PSV's opening-night record. Prediction: PSV 2-1 Shakhtar Donetsk.

                                            Fenerbahce vs Roma

                                            Fenerbahce vs Roma (+115 to +137 on Roma): Fenerbahce are in the competition proper for the first time since 2008/09 and have won their last three home matches against Italian clubs with clean sheets in all three. Roma are back for the first time since 2018/19, have won their last five against Turkish opposition, and have reached the knockout stage in seven of their last eight participations. The 22-cent spread on Roma between FanDuel and LetsBetMD is among the widest on any away favorite this week. BetRivers' Under 2.5 at -103 fits both sides' defensive records against each other's countries. Prediction: Fenerbahce 1-1 Roma.

                                            Como vs RB Leipzig

                                            Como vs RB Leipzig (-102 to -115 on Como): Como's first-ever UEFA fixture. They conceded a Serie A-low 29 goals last season and have already beaten Napoli away this term. Leipzig have not kept a clean sheet in their last 18 European matches and have won one of their last ten in the Champions League proper. The books make the debutants favorites, and rightly so. LetsBetMD's -102 on Como is the number; Leipzig at +270 on FanDuel is the best away price. Prediction: Como 2-1 RB Leipzig.

                                            Bayern Munich vs Bodo/Glimt

                                            Bayern Munich vs Bodo/Glimt (-714 to -900): Bayern have won their opening match in 22 straight Champions League campaigns and are unbeaten in 38 home group-stage or league-phase games since December 2013. One more goal makes them the second club to 900 in European Cup history. Harry Kane has 54 Champions League goals in 70 games. Bodo/Glimt have scored two or more in 11 of their last 12 UEFA matches and drew 2-2 at Dortmund last season, though they have never beaten a German side. The total sits at 4.5, and BetRivers' Over at -103 is the value given Bodo's scoring record and Bayern's ceiling. Prediction: Bayern Munich 4-1 Bodo/Glimt.

                                            Manchester United vs Sabah

                                            Manchester United vs Sabah (-670 to -900): United's 300th European Cup match, the first English club to the milestone, and their first Champions League appearance since 2023/24. Michael Carrick's side have won just one of their last seven group-stage matches. Sabah are Azerbaijan's second-ever league-phase club and won six of eight in qualifying. The 230-cent gap between FanDuel and BetRivers on United is the second-widest favorite spread on the board, and BetRivers' Over 3.5 at -143 is the play. Prediction: Manchester United 3-0 Sabah.

                                            Slavia Prague vs Lens

                                            Slavia Prague vs Lens (+135 to +150 on Lens): Slavia are winless in 19 Champions League proper matches since 2007. Lens have not played a goalless Champions League group game in 18 attempts and failed to score in just three of them. That is a pro-goals fixture on both sides, and BetRivers' Over 2.5 at -117 reflects it. Lens at +150 on BetRivers is 15 cents better than FanDuel. Prediction: Slavia Prague 1-2 Lens.

                                            Best Bets for Champions League Matchday 1

                                            1. Arsenal to beat Napoli (-118 at LetsBetMD) — Seven goals conceded in 15 matches last season, unbeaten in regulation, unbeaten on Matchday 1 in nine straight campaigns, and a 3-1 head-to-head edge. Napoli have won two of their last ten in the competition proper. Twelve cents better than FanDuel on the same outcome.

                                            2. Over 3.5 goals in Real Madrid vs Inter (+140 at BetRivers) — Mbappe chasing Raul, Inter scoring in 17 of their last 19 Champions League matches, and neither side playing for a draw. Plus money on four goals in the round's marquee fixture.

                                            3. Over 2.5 goals in Club Brugge vs Aston Villa (-148 at BetRivers) — Brugge have hit three or more in six of their last seven UEFA home games. Villa have conceded five in three league matches with Onana out. The clean-sheet stat is real, but the current form says goals.

                                            4. Longshot flier — Porto to beat Manchester City (+360 at BetRivers) — City have lost six of their last nine Champions League away games and 4-1 on their last visit to Portugal. Porto have lost one of their last 14 UEFA home matches. Small stakes, but the number should be shorter.

                                            Suggested three-leg parlay: Arsenal, Bayern and Real Madrid, all straight wins, which lands at roughly +210 to +240 depending on where each leg is placed. Build it leg by leg across the best betting apps rather than accepting one operator's parlay price.
