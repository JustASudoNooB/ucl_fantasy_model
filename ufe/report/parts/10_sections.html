How it works

Four steps turn fixtures into a team. Everything on this page comes out of the same simulation, so the numbers agree with each other.

Rate the clubsAttack and defence ratings from about 16,000 league and European matches, with recent games and stronger leagues counting for more. Bookmaker prices are blended in when they are on file.
Rate the playersEach player's share of his club's goals and assists (xG and xA per 90 where known), ball recoveries, and how likely he is to start, from line-up history, UEFA's availability flags and predicted line-ups.
Play every match 12,000 timesScorelines, starters, substitutions, scorers, assisters, clean sheets, recoveries, cards and the player of the match are drawn together, then scored with the game's rules.
Pick the teamAn optimiser finds the best 15 inside the budget and club limits, the starting XI and the captain, including when to switch captain or substitute between a matchday's days.

Reading the numbers

Expected points (xPts)The average of a player's points across all simulations, bench minutes and missed games included.
If he startsHis average in the simulations where he started. The gap to xPts is the price of rotation risk.
Start chanceHow often he is in the XI. Outfield players are capped at 85%, because players who looked certain started 83% of the time.
10+ and blankThe chance of a double-figure score, and of 2 points or fewer. Captains are picked for the first, benches judged by the second.
RangeThe 10th to 90th percentile of his points: four matchdays in five land inside it.
ValueExpected points per €1m of price.
OwnershipThe share of managers who own him in the official game. Low ownership with high xPts is a differential.
Clean sheetThe chance his club concedes nothing while he plays 60 minutes: 4 points for keepers and defenders, 1 for midfielders.

What it cannot know

Line-ups are confirmed about an hour before kick-off, after the deadline. Until then every start chance is an estimate, and it is the largest source of error. A single matchday is also mostly luck: across four graded matchdays the forecast explains about a quarter of the variation in players' points. Use it to tilt decisions, never as a guarantee.
