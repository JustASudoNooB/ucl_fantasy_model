What runs each matchday

  1. IngestFixtures and results, the official game feed (prices, positions, points), odds, Understat xG, team news. Every row keeps its source and fetch time.
  2. Team modelHierarchical Dixon–Coles model over ~11,000 matches in 13 leagues and three European seasons, time-decayed and tuned by backtest; blends in bookmaker odds when available.
  3. Line-ups and minutesStart probability per player from roles, prices and history; news cuts it, confirmed line-ups replace it about an hour before kick-off.
  4. Player eventsShares of team xG and xA, ball recoveries, saves, cards, penalties and own goals, each with its own rate.
  5. Joint simulationEvery match played thousands of times, goal by goal, so a keeper's clean sheet and his striker's goals move together.
  6. DecisionsInteger programming for squad, XI and transfers; an in-matchday policy for captain switches and manual subs; chip timing.
  7. Learning loopAfter each matchday: score the frozen pre-deadline predictions, update recovery calibration and per-player rates, refit, log the version.
